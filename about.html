Why We Created Engg.tv

The Fundamentals of Engineering (FE) exam is a critical milestone for any aspiring engineer. Yet, traditional preparation methods are often disjointed, uninspiring, and disconnected from the actual syllabus. Engg.tv was born out of a personal frustration with the lack of structured, high-quality, and modern study resources. We recognized that technology could be leveraged to transform a daunting preparation process into a streamlined, highly effective, and engaging journey.

"When I began studying for the FE Exam, I quickly realized there were surprisingly few guiding resources available—despite the exam's immense importance. This sparked a lifelong mission to build the platform I wish I had."
