Privacy Policy

At Engg.tv, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you prepare for the FE Exam using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information to provide a customized exam preparation experience:

  • Account Credentials: Your username, email address, and password when you register.
  • Preparation Goals: Your chosen engineering discipline (e.g., Mechanical, Civil, Electrical) and exam timeline.
  • Performance Data: Your quiz answers, scores, leaderboard position, and overall readiness progress.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is solely used to enhance your test preparation:

  • To synchronize your study progress, scores, and streak data across your desktop and mobile devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • To display leaderboard standings. For privacy, non-admin users only see email addresses masked up to the domain name.
  • To customize mock exam configurations tailored to your discipline.

3. Data Retention and Security

All user accounts and statistics are stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Firestore Database. We implement technical and administrative safeguards to protect your passwords and progress from unauthorized access. You can request deletion of your account and related study history at any time.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use standard web local storage (localStorage) to keep you securely signed in to the application and store current session states. No promotional or marketing trackers are used on this platform.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect platform updates or guidelines. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
